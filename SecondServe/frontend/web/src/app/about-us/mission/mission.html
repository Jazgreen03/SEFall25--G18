Our Mission

SecondServe is a revolutionary platform bridging the gap between food surplus and food insecurity. We empower local restaurants and grocery stores to donate non-retail, soon-to-expire food directly to families and individuals in need. Through an intuitive interface, users can view participating locations, schedule pickups or deliveries, and gain access to fresh, nutritious food that would otherwise go to waste.

Our mission is simple yet powerful — to reduce food waste, fight hunger, and strengthen community connections. Participating businesses not only make a positive social impact but also benefit from tax-deductible donations and recognition as sustainability leaders in their communities.

“Together, we can turn surplus into service.”
